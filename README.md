# Papiro

Welcome to Papiro, your expert multi-disciplinary academic companion and AI-powered smart notebook application for Android! Designed for students, researchers, and professionals, Papiro combines a rich Markdown editor, robust local storage, and powerful AI-driven features to elevate your note-taking and learning experience.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Rich Markdown Editor**: Write and format your notes with full Markdown support, including headings, lists, bold/italic text, custom styled tables, and syntax-highlighted code blocks.
* **AI Note Generation**: Instantly generate highly structured, conceptual overviews and comprehensive engineering/academic notes just by typing a topic.
* **AI Note Enhancement**: Use the AI Coprocessor to improve clarity, refine formatting, complete code snippets, and optimize content in your existing notes.
* **Interactive Quiz & Flashcard Generator**: Automatically convert your notes into interactive Multiple Choice quizzes or Flashcards, complete with a virtual AI Tutor to clarify concepts.
* **Mermaid Diagram Rendering**: Render dynamic flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state diagrams, and architectural graphs directly within your notes from Markdown blocks (` ```mermaid `).
* **Native Illustration Canvas**: Incorporate vector-based drawings and free-form sketches inside your notes using ` ```drawing ` blocks, featuring adjustable stroke thickness, eraser, and smart shape correction.
* **Mathematical Equations**: Seamlessly render complex scientific and mathematical formulas with LaTeX/KaTeX equation support (`$$E = mc^2$$`).
* **Smart Image Search & Retrieval**: Automatically resolve keyword-based image paths or placeholders into high-quality images via an optimized search engine, featuring proactive background pre-warmup to eliminate backend cold starts.
* **Whiteboard & Document OCR**: Snap photos of whiteboards or import PDFs and documents, with AI-driven extraction formatting text and structure directly into your note.
* **Note Version History**: Track changes with full temporal snapshotting, enabling you to inspect, compare, and restore previous iterations of your notes.
* **Auto Table of Contents**: Automatically generate a structured, interactive table of contents based on the headings in your note for seamless navigation.
* **Visual Customization**: Personalize your workspace with multiple elegant themes (Indigo, Cosmic, Matcha, Sakura), dark/light modes, and paper backdrops (Grid, Ruled, Dotted, Plain).
* **Google Gemini Integration**: Leverages Google Gemini models for lightning-fast AI processing, advanced contextual generation, and layout-aware document extraction.
* **Offline-First Storage**: All notes, drawing coordinates, and revision histories are stored securely on-device using a local Room database.
